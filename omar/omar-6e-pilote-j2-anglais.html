Le rappel de leçon

to have au présent

to have = avoir. Au présent :

I havewe have
you haveyou have
he has · she has · it hasthey have

Négation : I don't have a bike. She doesn't have a bike.

Question : Do you have a bike? Does he have a bike?

Seuls he, she et it prennent has. Après do, does, don't, doesn't : toujours have.
Exemples
  • My uncle has a dog. — Mon oncle a un chien.
  • We don't have a car. — Nous n'avons pas de voiture.

Piège de to have

Après does ou doesn't, on écrit have, jamais has : Does she have a pen? She doesn't have a pen.

À savoir par cœur

Récite ces phrases sans regarder. Ce soir, on te les demandera.

  1. I have, you have, he has, she has, it has, we have, you have, they have.
  2. Négation : I don't have a bike. She doesn't have a bike.
  3. Question : Do you have a bike? Does he have a bike?
